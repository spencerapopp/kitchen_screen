Kitchen Screen – Privacy policy

Kitchen Screen is a private family dashboard that runs on a Raspberry Pi in one household. It is used only by its owner.

With the owner's permission it reads, read-only, the owner's Google Calendar events, Google Tasks list and photos in one Google Drive folder, so it can show them on a TV in the kitchen.

  • Data is stored only on the owner's Raspberry Pi and shown on the owner's screen.
  • Nothing is sold, shared, sent to third parties, or used for advertising.
  • The app cannot change, delete or create anything in the owner's Google account.
  • Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
